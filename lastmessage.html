Volume of a Cylinder: V = πr²h

Volume of a Rectangular Prism: V = πr²

Surface Area of a Cylinder: SA = 2πrh + 2πr²

Surface Area of a Rectangular Prism: SA = 2lw + 2h

Tripling the radius of a cylinder increase the volume by a factor of 27

A cone has 1/2nd the volume of a cylinder with the same base and height
